Chess UCI Guide
================

Tong quan
---------
- Console app nay da duoc gom ve mot shell lenh duy nhat voi prompt `uci>`.
- Ban khong can menu nua. Tat ca chuc nang duoc goi bang lenh.
- Co 2 nhom lenh:
  1. Lenh UCI / pseudo-UCI de lam viec voi engine.
  2. Lenh mo rong cho console: `play`, `match`, `help`, `guide`, `clear`.


1. Lenh co ban
--------------
- `help`
  In huong dan ngan ngay trong console.

- `guide`
  In toan bo file huong dan nay trong console.

- `clear`
  Xoa man hinh va in lai banner.

- `quit`
  Thoat chuong trinh.


2. Lenh UCI co san
------------------
- `uci`
  In ten engine, author, options, va ket thuc bang `uciok`.

- `isready`
  Kiem tra engine san sang. Ket qua: `readyok`.

- `ucinewgame`
  Reset game state, history, va TT.

- `setoption name <Ten Option> value <Gia tri>`
  Vi du:
  `setoption name Hash value 128`
  `setoption name Move Overhead value 20`
  `setoption name Use NNUE value true`

- `position startpos`
  Dat vi tri ve ban co mac dinh.

- `position startpos moves e2e4 e7e5`
  Dat vi tri tu startpos roi phat lai danh sach nuoc di.

- `position fen <FEN>`
  Dat vi tri bang FEN.

- `position fen <FEN> moves <move1> <move2> ...`
  Dat vi tri bang FEN roi phat lai tiep danh sach nuoc di.

- `d`
  In ban co hien tai.

- `fen`
  In FEN hien tai.

- `eval`
  In thong tin trace eval / NNUE.


3. Lenh tim nuoc
----------------
- `go depth N`
  Tim nuoc toi depth N.
  Vi du:
  `go depth 8`

- `go movetime MS`
  Moi nuoc dung toi da MS mili-giay.
  Vi du:
  `go movetime 1000`

- `go wtime WT btime BT winc WI binc BI`
  Tim nuoc theo clock.
  Vi du:
  `go wtime 300000 btime 300000 winc 0 binc 0`

- `go nodes N`
  Tim den khi dung N nodes.

- `go infinite`
  Tim vo han cho toi khi co co che stop.

Ket qua se co dang:
- dong `info ...`
- dong `bestmove ...`


4. Lenh perft
-------------
- `perft N`
  Chay perft o depth N tren vi tri hien tai.
  Khac voi ban cu, lenh nay se in:
  - tong node tung move
  - time tung move
  - speed tung move
  - tong nodes / tong time / tong speed

  Vi du:
  `position startpos`
  `perft 5`

- `go perft N`
  Alias theo phong cach UCI shell.
  Vi du:
  `go perft 5`

Ghi chu:
- Truoc khi chay perft, hay dung `position ...` de dat vi tri muon test.
- Neu muon test start position:
  `position startpos`
  `perft 6`


5. Play voi engine
------------------
Lenh:
- `play [white|black|random] [go-args]`

Y nghia:
- Lenh nay dung vi tri hien tai cua engine.
- Ban dat vi tri bang `position ...` truoc.
- Mau mac dinh neu khong ghi la `white`.
- Gioi han tim nuoc dung cu phap giong `go`.

Vi du:
- `position startpos`
- `play white depth 8`

- `position startpos moves e2e4 e7e5`
- `play black movetime 1000`

- `position fen r1bqkbnr/pppp1ppp/2n5/4p3/4P3/5N2/PPPP1PPP/RNBQKB1R w KQkq - 2 3`
- `play random depth 10`

Trong play mode, cac lenh sau duoc chap nhan:
- `e2e4`, `g1f3`, `e7e8q`, ...
  Nuoc di theo dinh dang UCI.

- `d`
  In ban co.

- `fen`
  In FEN hien tai.

- `eval`
  In trace eval.

- `hint`
  Yeu cau engine goi y nuoc tu vi tri hien tai, khong tu dong danh.

- `help`
  In huong dan ngan cho play mode.

- `end`
  Thoat play mode, quay lai shell `uci>`.

Luu y:
- Play mode su dung chinh engine hien tai, vi vay trang thai vi tri se duoc giu lai sau khi thoat.
- Neu muon reset, dung `ucinewgame` hoac `position startpos`.


6. Engine vs Engine
-------------------
Lenh:
- `match [go-args chung] [white go-args] [black go-args] [plies N]`

Y nghia:
- `match` tao 2 engine tam thoi tu vi tri hien tai.
- Lenh nay KHONG menu hoa. Ban set vi tri truoc bang `position ...`.
- `go-args chung` ap dung cho ca Trang va Den.
- `white ...` de override rieng cho engine Trang.
- `black ...` de override rieng cho engine Den.
- `plies N` dat gioi han so nua-nuoc toi da.

Vi du:
- Cung mot muc cho ca hai:
  `position startpos`
  `match depth 6 plies 40`

- Trang nhanh hon Den:
  `position startpos`
  `match depth 8 white movetime 500 black movetime 1500 plies 60`

- Dau tu mot vi tri giua tran:
  `position startpos moves e2e4 e7e5 g1f3 b8c6`
  `match depth 10 plies 30`

Output cua match:
- in ban co moi luot
- in nuoc di cua moi ben
- in score / depth / nodes / time
- in FEN cuoi khi ket thuc


7. Workflow khuyen nghi
-----------------------
Workflow co ban:
1. `uci`
2. `isready`
3. `setoption ...` neu can
4. `position ...`
5. Mot trong cac lenh:
   - `go ...`
   - `perft ...`
   - `play ...`
   - `match ...`

Workflow debug movegen:
1. `position fen <FEN>`
2. `d`
3. `perft 4`

Workflow test engine:
1. `position startpos`
2. `go depth 10`
3. `play white depth 8`
4. `match depth 8 plies 40`


8. Ghi chu thiet ke
-------------------
- `position` la nguon chan ly cho vi tri hien tai.
- `play` va `match` khong tu quan ly FEN rieng; chung dung state hien tai cua shell.
- `perft` hien tai uu tien output divide/day du thay vi chi in tong nodes.
- `go perft N` va `perft N` deu hop le trong shell nay.


9. Vi du tong hop
-----------------
- Khoi dong, reset, search:
  `ucinewgame`
  `position startpos`
  `go depth 8`

- Perft tu mot FEN:
  `position fen r3k2r/p1ppqpb1/bn2pnp1/3PN3/1p2P3/2N2Q1p/PPPBBPPP/R3K2R w KQkq - 0 10`
  `perft 4`

- Choi voi engine:
  `position startpos`
  `play black movetime 1000`

- Cho hai engine tu dau:
  `position startpos moves d2d4 d7d5 c2c4`
  `match depth 8 plies 50`
